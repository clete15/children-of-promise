Active children only, highest score first. Points are the total printed on the child’s filed Weighted Eligibility form. Where that form cannot be read (a scan, for example), the points are worked out from the records and marked with *. Records support is what a form rebuilt today would score, from the pre-enrollment answers, then the enrollment record, then the Parent Interview (only where it asks the same question).
