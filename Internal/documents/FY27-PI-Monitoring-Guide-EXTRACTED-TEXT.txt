What is the
PIQET
-
3
?
Developed, updated, and maintained by Vander Weele Group on behalf of the Illinois Department of Early
Childhood, t
he PIQET
-
3
supports
the administration and monitoring of the Prevention Initiative Early
Childhood Block Grant program.
This tool provides valuable insights into the effective operation of high
-
quality programs, going beyond basic compliance to examine key organizational factors that contribute to
success.
A copy of the PIQET
-
3
and scoring information can be found on the IDEC Prevention Initiative
website
.
T
he PIQET
-
3
utilizes a multi-
method
approach to data collection:
Supervisor
Interviews:
Interviews with program administrators provide qualitative information
on leadership, organizational climate, and program implementation.
Staff Surveys:
Brief
,
online
,
anonymous
surveys distributed to staff members gather qualitative
data on their perspectives and experiences within the program.
Documentation:
Review of some documents provided for compliance review and a few more
documents specifically for the PIQET-
3
.
The
PIQET
-
3
examines four critical areas:
Functional Organizational Climate:
Evaluating the effectiveness of data systems, financial
management, and program planning.
Curriculum and Service Delivery:
Assessing fidelity to the chosen curriculum or program model
and how well programs meet the individual needs of children and families.
Family and Community Engagement:
Examining family participation, referral processes, and
collaborative relationships within the community.
Implementation Leadership:
Analyzing the effectiveness of leadership, staff support, performance
assessment, and employee morale and retention.
The PIQET
-
3 report highlights key strengths and opportunities for growth, helping programs identify
targeted areas for quality improvement
.

To prepare for your home visit observation, please review the following guidelines:
Visits to Avoid: Do not submit recordings of first visits, prenatal visits, or visits where a
developmental screening, Individualized Family Service Plan review, or similar activity is
planned.

Virtual Visits:
Recordings of virtual home visits are acceptable for submission if they adhere to the

Previously Recorded Visits:
You may use a previously recorded home visit if it was recorded
within the last six months
of the preparation meeting

Recording Length:
The recording should capture the entire home visit from start to finish and be
at least 60 minutes long to ensure assessors can observe significant events during greeting and
departure.
Uploading a shorter video is at the Grantees discretion.
Focus on Interaction:
The purpose of the observation is to see how the home visitor interacts with
the family. The home visitor, child(ren), and caregiver(s) should all be visible in the recording.
Supervisor Review:
Before submitting the recording, have a supervisor review it to ensure it
meets the guidelines and has no technical issues with sound or video.
Typical Visit:
Choose a typical home visit that shows the parent and child interacting with each
other and the home visitor. Obtaining Consent for Home Visit Recordings
