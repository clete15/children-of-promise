Dear Parent or Guardian of ,

As prepares to move on from our Preschool for All program to kindergarten in the school year, we want to support a smooth transition for your family. Our transition plan includes:

• Sharing your child's assessment summaries and portfolio with you.
• Providing information about kindergarten registration and your local school.
• Offering a family conference to discuss your child's readiness and any supports.
• Forwarding records, with your written consent, to the receiving school.

Children of Promise prohibits expulsion and suspension of preschool children (23 Ill. Adm. Code 235.320); we partner with families on any behavioral support a child needs.

Please reach out with any questions. We are proud to have been part of your child's early learning.

Warm regards,
