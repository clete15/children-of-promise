Policy Purpose & Access
This salary scale establishes fair, transparent, and equitable compensation across all positions at Children of Promise LLC. Salary floors and pay tiers are determined by job responsibilities, role classification, required educational qualifications, and specialized training/licensure.

Access: The general salary scale (excluding administrative compensation) is accessible to all staff members via the Employee Handbook. This complete document including administrative salaries is available to management upon request. It is reviewed annually for internal equity and local market alignment.

Placement & Progression Criteria

Educational & Credentials

Base pay and movement within a tier are influenced by verified qualifications:

  • Tier 2 Preschool Teacher (PEL) requires a 4-year Bachelor's degree and active Professional Educator License
  • Tier 2 Lead Teacher requires an Associate's degree or equivalent ECE credential/experience
  • Tier 1 Administration requires meeting state/Gateways Director qualifications
  • All positions consider verified ECE credit hours, degrees, and active Gateways credentials

Probationary Period

Assistant Teachers (Tier 4) start at $17.25/hr during the 90-day introductory/probationary period. Upon successful completion and satisfactory performance evaluation, rate increases to $18.00/hr.

Experience & Merit

Performance reviews determine eligibility for merit-based wage increases above the baseline pay floor. Increases are not automatic or guaranteed. When management determines that funding and organizational needs allow, merit-based increases may be considered at the Director's discretion.

Part-Time Positions

Part-time staff are compensated at the hourly rate assigned to their job classification tier, with accrued leave prorated based on scheduled hours worked.

Overtime

All employees (exempt and non-exempt) are paid 1.5x their regular hourly rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek.

Salary Scale Criteria Summary

This salary scale is based on:

  • Different Roles: Tiers 1–4 reflect distinct responsibilities and pay ranges
  • General Education: Bachelor's degree → Associate's → High School Diploma
  • Specialized Training: Gateways credentials, PEL, Paraprofessional License, ECE coursework
  • Years of Relevant Experience: Placement within pay range considers ECE/childcare experience
  • Professional Credentials: Active licensure determines eligibility for specific tier positions
