Lead Teacher (LT): The individual with the highest professional qualifications assigned to teach a group of children and who is responsible for daily lesson planning, parent conferences, child assessment, and curriculum planning.

Teacher (T): A member of the teaching team who shares responsibility with the Lead Teacher for the care and education of an assigned group of children.

Assistant Teacher/Aide (AT): A member of the teaching team assigned to a group of children who works under the direct supervision of the Lead Teacher and/or Teacher.
