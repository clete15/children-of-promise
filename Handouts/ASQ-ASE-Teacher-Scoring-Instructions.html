ASQ-3 & ASQ:SE-2 — Updated Process

Scoring & signatures for Prevention Initiative (PI) children

Children of Promise Christian Dayschool · Millstadt, IL

What's changing: Parents still fill out the ASQ-3 / ASQ:SE-2 questionnaire. Going forward, do not send the summary page home for the parent to score — the teacher enters the summary scoring in our system. We also need signatures on every PI child's forms on the Lenovo IdeaTab, even if the family already signed.

A PI child's ASQ / ASE is complete (green) only when the questionnaire is uploaded, the scores are entered, and the summary is signed by both parent and staff. A teacher-administered summary is compliant for PICC/monitoring (PI10). If any older ASQ handout still says the parent scores the summary, update or retire it so our materials match this process.
