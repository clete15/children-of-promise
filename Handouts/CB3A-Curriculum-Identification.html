Children of Promise Christian Dayschool · Millstadt, IL · DCFS Licensed Center-Based Prevention Initiative

Statement

Children of Promise implements The Creative Curriculum (Teaching Strategies, LLC) as its research-based, child-centered curriculum, together with Teaching Strategies GOLD as the aligned authentic assessment. Both are delivered through the Teaching Strategies online platform, where the curriculum is reached under the Teach area and the assessment under the Assess area. The Creative Curriculum is the curriculum designed to be used with the GOLD assessment, so the curriculum and the assessment required by CB3 are a matched pair rather than two unrelated products.

How this shows in practice (CB3.B)

Teachers build each weekly lesson plan in the Teach area, so the plan is drawn from the Creative Curriculum’s own content — its studies, Intentional Teaching Experiences and Mighty Minutes — rather than assembled independently. The child activities on the printed plan therefore carry the curriculum’s objectives. GOLD documentation gathered in the Assess area informs the following week’s planning, which is the curriculum-to-assessment link CB3 asks for.

The lesson plans themselves are filed under CB3.B Lesson Plans.
