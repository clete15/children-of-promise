PI Classroom Daily Schedules

The 9:30 to 12:00 Prevention Initiative block — draft for teacher review

Children of Promise Christian Dayschool · Millstadt, IL

This is a draft, not a record of what you do. Mark it up and give it back — the posted schedule has to match what actually happens in the room, because the monitor compares the two. The PI block is exactly the two and a half hours we are required to provide, so each one starts at 9:30 and runs to 12:00 with nothing spare.

Infants / Toddlers PI

Room 2 · 8–20 months
Paige & Sue
TimeWhat happens
Throughout Feeding, diapering and sleeping follow each baby’s own rhythm Not on the clock. Each child’s pattern is on their individual care chart, and the schedule below bends around it.
9:30 Hello and settling in Greet each baby and family by name at their level. Soft start on the floor.
9:45 Floor time and exploration Tummy time, reaching and grasping, treasure baskets, board books, mirrors, soft blocks. Adults down on the floor, naming what each baby is doing.
10:15 Morning bottles and snack Held and talked with for bottles. Finger food at the table for the older ones.
10:40 Songs, rhymes and books One to one or two or three at a time, never the whole room at once. Repeat favorites.
11:00 Outside or stroller walk Blanket on the grass, push toys for the walkers. Indoor gross motor if the weather is against us.
11:30 Sensory and messy play Water, scarves, textures, safe household objects. Named out loud throughout.
11:50 Quiet down and lunch transition Soft music, cuddles and books while lunch is set up.
12:00 PI block ends

Nothing here is a fixed group activity. With babies this young, the adults follow the children and the times are a guide to what is offered, not a bell.

Toddlers PI

Room 3 · 15–24 months
Lindsey
TimeWhat happens
Throughout Diapering, toileting and handwashing as needed Plus water offered freely all morning.
9:30 Arrival and hello song Each child greeted by name. Short and sung, on the rug.
9:40 Free choice Blocks, books, pretend play, art, fine motor. Adults spread out across the room, joining in and talking about what children are doing.
10:20 Snack At the table together, served family style where we can. Names of foods, colors and counting as it happens.
10:40 Story and songs Two short books with a small group. Fingerplays and movement in between.
11:00 Outside Climbing, balls, push and ride toys, digging. Gross motor indoors if the weather keeps us in.
11:35 Small group activity One short planned experience from the week’s plan — sensory, music, painting or sorting — offered to a few children at a time.
11:50 Tidy up and lunch transition Children help put things away. Books while lunch arrives.
12:00 PI block ends

2 Year Olds PI

Room 5 · 24–36 months
Molly & Renee
TimeWhat happens
Throughout Toileting, diapering and handwashing as needed Plus water available all morning.
9:30 Arrival and morning meeting Hello song, weather, who is here today. Kept to about ten minutes.
9:45 Free choice in the centers Blocks, pretend play, books, art, sand or water, puzzles and fine motor. Adults move between areas asking questions and extending play.
10:30 Snack Children help set up and pour. Conversation at the table.
10:50 Story and music or movement A book with the group, then dancing, instruments or a movement game.
11:10 Outside Climbing, riding toys, balls, sand, water. Gross motor indoors in bad weather.
11:40 Small group activity The week’s planned experience with a few children at a time — art, science, cooking, sorting or counting.
11:55 Tidy up and lunch transition Children put materials away, wash hands, choose a book.
12:00 PI block ends
